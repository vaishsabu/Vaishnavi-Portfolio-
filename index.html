Product concepts in progress

These are proposed concepts—not launched products or completed case studies. The next step is to validate the problem with real users.

Concept 01To validate

QueueLess

Problem hypothesis: People lose time and confidence when public-service queues and appointment availability are unclear.

Potential MVP: Service discovery, appointment booking, queue position and estimated wait updates.

Validation plan: Interview service users and staff; map the current journey; test a clickable prototype.

Possible measures: wait-time variance · No-show rate · Task completion · User satisfaction
Concept 02To validate

FocusFlow

Problem hypothesis: Teams struggle to keep priorities, decisions and owners visible across ongoing work.

Potential MVP: Shared priorities, lightweight decision log, owners and follow-up tracking.

Validation plan: Interview cross-functional teams; audit existing workarounds; test adoption with a small pilot.

Possible measures: Weekly active teams · Decision follow-through · Time to alignment
